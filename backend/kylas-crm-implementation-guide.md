## The 5-Step Implementation Checklist

### 1. Discovery and Workflow Mapping
Define your deal stages. Are you B2B with long sales cycles, or B2C with high-volume transactions? Mapping these stages prevents "shelfware" by ensuring the CRM reflects reality.

### 2. Data Hygiene and Scrubbing
"Garbage In, Garbage Out" is the leading cause of CRM failure. 
*   Remove duplicate entries.
*   Standardize phone number formats (e.g., +1 or +91).
*   Map custom fields (e.g., "Industry Type" or "Lead Source").

### 3. Activating Conversational Commerce
Integration with the **WhatsApp Business API** is the cornerstone of modern CRM. This moves your team away from fragmented personal phone threads into a centralized timeline where every interaction is recorded.

### 4. No-Code Automations
Set up "Smart Leaks" to capture leads from Facebook, Google Ads, and your website. Use **Zapier** or **Pabbly** to automate lead distribution so that reps are notified instantly when a high-intent prospect interacts with your brand.

### 5. Training and Onboarding
Kylas provides human-led onboarding. Ensure your team is trained on the mobile app, as field agents who update lead status in real-time contribute to a **42% improvement in forecast accuracy**.
